How the linked shop flow works

01

Customer orders here

Your customer adds products to the cart and fills in shipping details on this website.

02

Supplier link opens

The order button opens the connected supplier URL and copies the customer order summary.

03

Production upgrade

For true automatic ordering, connect a backend to a supplier API, Shopify app, WooCommerce REST API, or dropshipping service that allows programmatic checkout.

Important: a static website cannot securely place paid orders on another shop by itself. This front-end is ready for supplier handoff now, and the next step is adding approved API credentials on a secure server.
